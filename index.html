The Franklin Fellowship

A student-led initiative to ignite curiosity for chemistry among passionate, under-resourced learners and show them the path to research.

What We Stand For

The Franklin Fellowship is named after two scientists who embody what this program stands for. Benjamin Franklin, despite being born into poverty, rose through curiosity to become one of history's great polymaths — a scientist,and founding father. Rosalind Franklin, the brilliant chemist whose work was key to uncovering the structure of DNA, was long denied the recognition she deserved. This fellowship honors both: those who start with little, those who go unseen, and those who dare to keep asking why. Through it, we hope to enlighten the next Benjamin Franklin and the next Rosalind Franklin — giving underrepresented and disadvantaged students who have the potential, like them, a chance to be seen.

Mission

The Franklin Fellowship seeks to spark a lifelong curiosity for Chemistry in bright students from disadvantaged backgrounds. We believe that seeing the beauty of atoms, molecules and experiments — and meeting future scientists and researchers(like you!) — transforms the idea of "a career in science" from distant to possible.

Participate

Students with a strong interest in chemistry — regardless of past grades — are welcome.

To express interest, visit the Apply page or write to vihaanagrawal@proton.me.

The only requirements to participate are high school chemistry knowledge, a willingness to work hard and be challenged, and, above all, the desire to learn.

Meet the Team

We are a bunch of students who really like our subject and want to spread it further. Our team includes volunteers from diverse research backgrounds who understand how early mentorship can change a life.
